Game Developer

Being a game developer requires an understanding of computing systems and knowing what to expect in the field, as well as various skills and certifications to show your understanding of the career. The best start to being a game developer is getting a worthwhile education, preferably a bachelor's degree, and working your way from there.

What Do They Do?

Game developers work in tandem to produce a digital experience for users to enjoy. This involves designing graphics, coding mechanics, and creating ideas for later designs or additions. Game developers don't all do the same things, so the process of game design on a given day generally looks like this:

  • Implement and refine code
  • Create game graphics
  • Design levels
  • Playtest their games

Skills Needed

Technical Skills

  • Systems and Computing Skills
  • Problem-solving
  • Time Management

People Skills

  • Communication
  • Creativity

Getting Started

  1. Educate toward a bachelor's degree
  2. Experience various scripting systems
  3. Develop soft skills
  4. Develop technical skills

Certifications and Credentials

The Unity Certified Developer certification validates your ability to use and create experiences with Unity. The Unreal Engine Blueprint certification demonstrates proficiency with systems in Unreal Engine. You can find these listed on teal's Career Paths webpage.

Pay and Job Outlook

Salaries range from around $48-114k annually, based on experience and level of education, according to the New England Institute of Technology.

Who's Hiring a Game Developer?

Many companies are constantly searching for game developers, such as Electronic Arts, Microsoft, Epic Games, and Unity to name a few.

Why I chose to be a Game Developer

Game development has interested me from a young age, and developing a game uniquely your own offers an experience for others who share your preferences. Along with this, the technical aspect of understanding what happens behind the scenes in seemingly simple games is something I have always wanted to get into. There are many challenges that come with this career, and taking them on is something I never stopped looking forward to doing.
